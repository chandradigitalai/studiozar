About Me

The person behind StudioZar

I'm Chandra Nandipati, a Digital Marketing Specialist and Web Developer based in Tirupati. I started StudioZar with a simple belief - every business, big or small, deserves a strong online presence that actually brings in customers.

I specialize in building custom websites, optimizing them for search engines, running Google Ads, and creating content and branding that helps businesses stand out. From schools and clinics to gyms and startups, I've helped a range of clients attract leads and grow online.

I combine design, technology, SEO, and marketing to deliver results - not just pretty pages. When you work with me, you get a partner who cares about your growth.
